# Licensing decision

Status: Founder decision required

The repository remains `UNLICENSED`; no permission to copy, redistribute, or create
derivative works is granted. Selecting private/proprietary, source-available, or an
open-source license changes AION's contribution and distribution model and cannot be
inferred as an engineering detail.

Before any external distribution, the founder must choose the intended model and
approve the corresponding `LICENSE`, contributor terms, and third-party notice policy.
