Status: Accepted

## Decision

Sprint 3 Phase 7 is approved. Its explicit Career Evidence catalogue, deterministic structured
CareerFacts, conflict and supersession history, evidence-backed CareerProfile derivation,
RelationshipObject provenance/membership, bounded filesystem recovery model, tests,
documentation, pushed commit, backup, and isolated restore are accepted. No real owner career
data, permanent Career Object, real Identity value, or AI-assistant archive was read or persisted.

Sprint 3 Phase 8 is separately authorized by Founder directive
`AION-S3-P8-JOB-POSTING-IMPORT`. That authorization is limited to deterministic import of one
explicitly owner-supplied structured JSON, Markdown, or text Job Posting; a closed versioned
JobPosting payload; dry run; explicit create/revision operations; currentness evidence; bounded
Object persistence; and neutral synthetic proof.

## Preserved boundaries

- Phase 9 matching, scoring, ranking, drafting, job search, and job-board access remain unauthorized.
- Real Job Posting and owner career data ingestion remain unauthorized during Phase 8 implementation.
- Source references remain inert; no browser, fetch, scrape, network, model, telemetry, database, or
  vector-store behavior is authorized.
- AI-assistant archives and exports remain unapproved for access.
- DG-3 remains Open; tests are not normative fixtures.
- DG-4b remains Open; bounded synthetic evidence is not production workload evidence.
- The Universal Object Contract remains Pre-stable.

This decision preserves all earlier decisions and does not authorize Phase 9 or a Stable Object claim.
